02 Features

Everything you loved about the classics, with a few new tricks.

Tight controls

Coyote time, jump buffering and variable jump height. Every miss is your fault, not the game's.

Secrets everywhere

Hidden rooms, floating coin trails and bonus stages for players who look off the beaten path.

Boss battles

Each world ends with a hand-crafted boss that learns your patterns as the fight goes on.

Chiptune soundtrack

An original 8-bit score made with real square, triangle and noise channels.

Couch co-op

Grab a friend and a second controller. Shared lives, double the chaos.

Level editor

Build your own stages tile by tile and share them with a short code.

05 FAQ

Is the demo free?

Yes. The demo runs in any modern browser with no download and no account.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. On touch screens, on-screen buttons appear under the game. Landscape works best.

Which platforms will the full game be on?

Windows, macOS, Linux and the web first, with consoles to follow.

Is my high score saved?

Your best score is kept in this browser only. Clearing site data resets it.
